Deep Learning Development Process at Q2M Solutions

A well-defined, structured, and comprehensive approach for deep learning helps ensure accuracy and real-world application for each project. Each step in the process is designed to improve the quality of the final solution.

  1. 01

    Discovery and Review of Use Cases

    Our analysis of deep learning models begins with an understanding of business challenges, the target audience, the business goals, and the data type available. This identification helps us to outline the right project development approach.

  2. 02

    Data Assessment and Preparation

    The Q2M team of experts then assesses the quality, type, and use of existing data to ensure it is accurate, updated, structured, and properly labelled. This approach of preparing the data is essential to ensure a useful model quality and performance.

  3. 03

    Model Architecture and Selection

    Once the data and objective of the model are defined, we then plan the architecture to ensure the right model use case. This choice can involve selecting the right model between recurrent networks, convolutional neural networks, transformers, and other deep learning solutions.

  4. 04

    Training and Fine-Tuning

    We train the model to align with your business needs and refine it using experimentation, parameter tuning, and validation. This fosters overall model performance, ensuring reliable and accurate solution delivery.

  5. 05

    Testing and Validation

    Before deploying the model, we test it against real-world scenarios and validate it using the gathered business data. This helps ensure that the solution performs as per the expectations.

  6. 06

    Deploying and Integrating

    The designed model is finally integrated into the real-world business environment to test its performance and business outcomes. We also ensure to train your team and ensure seamless integration of the product, model, or internal systems with your existing data infrastructure and platforms.

  7. 07

    Monitoring and Iterating

    After deployment, we evaluate the performance based on user feedback and pre-defined metrics and iterate deep learning solutions accordingly. This helps keep the model relevant and useful with evolving and scaling business growth.
