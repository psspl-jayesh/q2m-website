What the apps do

Six building blocks of a smart glasses app

Most business apps for smart glasses combine a handful of these. The right mix depends on the job, so we start from the workflow, not the feature list.

Guided workflows

Step-by-step checklists and instructions in view or read aloud. The wearer moves on by voice, tap or gesture, without putting tools down.

Capture as you work

Photos, short videos and voice notes saved to the right job, asset or claim, with time and location attached.

Remote expert

A live call where a senior engineer sees the wearer's view, talks them through the problem and marks up the scene.

Vision AI

Models that read labels, serial numbers and gauges, or flag likely damage, so the wearer confirms instead of typing.

Voice assistant

Answers from your own manuals and SOPs: torque values, part numbers, safety steps, spoken back in seconds.

Back-office sync

Results written to your job, asset, claim or quality system, so the report is complete before the wearer leaves site.

How we build

A smart glasses solution has four parts

The app on the glasses is only the first. We design all four together so the job works end to end.

  1. 01

    Glasses app

    Shows steps, takes voice commands and captures media, with minimal screens and a voice option for every action.

  2. 02

    Phone or edge companion

    Handles connectivity, heavier processing and storage, and keeps the job running when the network drops.

  3. 03

    AI services

    Speech recognition, vision models and an assistant grounded in your manuals, in your cloud where data rules require it.

  4. 04

    Your systems

    The job, asset, claim, quality or patient record receives the results through existing APIs.

We design the workflow with the people who do the job before we build screens. A short ride-along on site changes more of the design than a week of meetings.

What a pilot looks like

Small enough to run fast, real enough to prove the case

  1. 01

    Pick one workflow

    A frequent, procedure-driven job, such as vehicle intake or a monthly equipment check. We agree the measures with the team lead.

  2. 02

    Test devices on site

    Two or three devices tried by the actual team in actual conditions. They pick the one they will wear for a full task.

  3. 03

    Build and run the pilot

    The app for that workflow, connected to one back-office system, run with a small group of devices over several weeks.

  4. 04

    Decide and scale

    Review the numbers with the team, fix what slowed people down, and plan the roll-out to more teams.

Plan for the real world

Practical things we design for from day one

Privacy and consent

Clear rules on when recording is on, a visible indicator, and consent steps for customers and patients.

Battery and shifts

Charging points, spare devices or phone-based processing, so a device lasts the task, not just the demo.

Connectivity

Offline steps for hangars, basements and plants, with sync when the device reconnects.

Safety

Overlays stay small and never block what the wearer needs to see. Site rules for eye protection come first.

Comfort and fit

Weight, heat and prescription lenses decide whether people keep the glasses on. We test with the real team.

Device management

How devices are charged, updated, assigned and wiped, and who supports them on site.

Measuring results

Measure the job, not the technology

We record a baseline before the pilot and use the same method afterwards, so the decision to scale rests on your own numbers.

  • Time per job

    From start of inspection to a completed report.

  • First-time completeness

    Reports that need no follow-up for missing photos or fields.

  • Expert travel

    Site visits replaced by remote calls.

  • Adoption

    How often people choose to wear the glasses when they have the option.

FAQ

Smart glasses app development questions

What is smart glasses app development?+

It is the design and build of software that runs on smart glasses: guided steps, photo and voice capture, remote expert calls and AI that reads the scene, plus the connection to your business systems.

Which smart glasses do you develop apps for?+

Meta, XREAL, Snap Spectacles, MentraOS, Android XR, Samsung XR and RayNeo. We choose the device per task after testing it with your team on site.

How much does a smart glasses app cost?+

It depends on the workflow, the number of systems to connect and the devices. We give a clear pilot quote after on-site discovery, so you know the cost before you commit.

Do smart glasses apps work offline?+

Yes. Key steps run offline and sync photos, notes and results when the device or paired phone reconnects.

How do you handle privacy with camera glasses?+

Recording is limited to the steps that need it, with a visible indicator, consent steps where needed, and storage in your own cloud under your access rules.
