The 30-minute session

How a 30-minute session works

No slides about AI in general. We talk about one process in your business.

First 10 minutes

You walk us through one process

Who does it today, which systems it touches, how many cases you handle and where time or errors pile up.

Next 15 minutes

We map the AI use case

An agent, an automation or a glasses workflow. We note the data and approvals it would need.

Last 5 minutes

You leave with next steps

A rough scope, the data we would need and a suggested pilot. We follow up within a week.
