Types of AI Agents Q2M Build for Your Business

Businesses have various needs; we identify them and build AI agents that are best suited for them, ranging from basic automated systems to highly adaptable AI models. Here’s how we scale agentic AI for measurable ROI across your enterprise with different types of AI agents:

01

Goal-Oriented Agents

For businesses and industries where strategic thinking, adaptability, and a clear sense of direction are important, goal-based AI agents work best. These agents are solutions-oriented; they operate by outlining possible features and then choosing the actions that help them move closer to a specific goal.

02

Learning Agents

Learning agents learn from interactions, essential in fields where chatbots and social media play a crucial role. We leverage natural language processing to build learning agents, so that they can analyse the behaviour of users and provide them with content recommendations. With time, it learns which actions are gaining rewards, and then it works on refining its approach.

03

Utility-Based Agents

Our AI developers build utility-based agents that are tailored to the specific problem of your business, where trade-offs matter. It works by following a consistent internal loop that makes it different from a goal-based agent. The utility-based agent can be used in healthcare, robotics, pricing systems, logistics, supply, self-driving cars, smart homes, and supply chain automation. These agents are resilient, and they can navigate complex problems and changing environments while delivering consistent results.

04

Autonomous Agents

An advanced form of AI is known as an autonomous agent, which is capable of understanding inquiries and then responding to them without taking any help from humans. These agents are built in a way that they first perceive information from their environment, using data sensors. Then, this information is used to take the next step. After receiving feedback, they learn from the results and adjust their future behaviour.

05

Hybrid Agents

A hybrid agent combines two different decision-making approaches in one system- typically rule-based/symbolic logic for predictable situations, and machine learning or utility-based reasoning for nuanced ones. Businesses leveraging hybrid AI agents are able to improve their productivity and compliance, achieving automation that delivers faster and more accurate business operations.

How Agentic AI Works?

Agentic AI goes beyond automation; it thinks, works with others, and takes initiative to get things done. Here is a detailed description of how it operates:

  • Reasoning The agentic AI begins by understanding the issue at hand. It analyses available data, weighs different options, and discovers the best path forward. Basically, it mirrors how a human would think and act while planning a task. This isn't just following pre-set rules; the system evaluates context, anticipates outcomes, and makes informed decisions on its own.
  • Collaboration No system works in isolation; in the same way, Agentic AI is built to coordinate, whether that means pulling information from other tools, working alongside human teams, or syncing with other AI agents to divide and conquer complex tasks. This collaborative layer ensures that the AI doesn't just operate independently but integrates smoothly into existing workflows and systems.
  • Action Once the reasoning through the problem and collaboration where needed are done, AI does its work, such as executing a task, triggering a process, or making a decision that moves the project forward. Without the need for human intervention at any stage.
