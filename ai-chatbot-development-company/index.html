Q2M's AI Chatbot Capabilities: From Conversation to Action

The AI chatbots we develop are based on many capabilities, all of which have been chosen because they solve a true business problem, and not just because they tick off items from a checklist that a software vendor supplies. Here's what powers the chatbots we build:

  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) All responses are based on your own documents, SharePoint data, and knowledge bases – not a generic model’s general training. Users receive precise answers that are consistent with your current policies and products and are properly cited.
  • Multi-Turn Conversational Memory The chatbot is context-aware throughout the conversation – thus saving user time by eliminating repetition and processing complicated multi-stage queries in one session, instead of several.
  • Intent Recognition & Entity Extraction State-of-the-art NLU understands the user’s intent, extracts all the necessary information (date, order number, etc.) and appropriately routes the conversation, even when users ask questions in different ways each time.
  • Live System Integration & Action Chatbots can do more than answer questions — they can perform actions. Raise tickets, balance checks, schedule appointments, maintain records, and initiate approvals. They are fully traceable, trackable, and reversible when required.
  • Sentiment Detection & Smart Escalation When a user is frustrated or the query is genuinely beyond the chatbot's scope, the system detects this and escalates to a human with full conversation context — not a cold transfer with no history.
  • Multilingual Support It serves users in English, Chinese, Malay, Tamil, and other languages — with detection of the language and responses in the language the user prefers, using Azure Cognitive Services for translation.
  • Conversation Analytics Dashboard Every conversation is tracked, rated by resolution quality, and available in a reporting layer ready to be acted upon by your team. You know exactly what your customers are asking, how your chatbot falls short, and what needs to improve – all presented to you in clear terms rather than raw logs.
  • Secure-by-Design Architecture Use of Azure Private Endpoints, managed identities, RBAC for conversations, no persistent PII in the chatbot logs, full control over data residency, and built-in PDPA/GDPR compliance from the architecture stage onward.
  • Omnichannel Deployment Deploy to web widget, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp Business, mobile app using SDK, or a complete custom brand experience. We work where your customers already live without forcing them to alter their behaviour to talk to your chatbot.
  • Continuous Model Improvement Every project we work on includes a 90-day optimisation period. Real user issues expose problems testing never uncovers. Retrain, tune, and iterate. The chatbot you’ll have at 90 days will be demonstrably better than when you deployed it.
  • Voice-to-Text & Speech Interfaces In applications like contact centres or interactive voice response (IVR), our chatbots accept spoken input using Azure Speech Services, allowing for voice interactions in AI over the telephone using the same NLU accuracy as text.
  • AI Ethics & Responsible Deployment Our chatbots are designed with safeguards against generating false or dangerous information and always respond in scope. Vital in highly regulated industries where a wrong answer means something.
