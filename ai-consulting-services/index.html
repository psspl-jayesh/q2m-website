01

Workforce Efficiency

Enterprises are facing a growing skill gap as they lose experienced mainframe developers as they retire, and new developers are not yet ready to fill the gap. Enterprises that embrace the use of AI have the potential to enhance business efficiency, improve decision-making, and drive innovation. AI can automatically convert old code into modern code, create documentation for legacy systems, and map out how old systems work. While AI-assisted tools can also reverse-engineer old logic to understand how it works, define the boundaries of each application. Training new developers becomes easy and quick with this approach.

02

Application Efficiency

Banks, insurers, and large enterprises still run their important operations on decades-old legacy systems. These systems were built long ago, before AI and analytics tools existed. As a result they valuable data was stored in rigid, proprietary formats that advanced tools can't read or process easily. These issues are creating big business problems, especially for those enterprises looking to evolve by adopting AI. How Q2M solves this issue: We connect the old data to new analytics instead of replacing the entire legacy systems, and use AI tools that act as a translation layer. They pull out all the key data and feed it into modern analytics platforms, dashboards, or cloud data warehouses without needing a full system overhaul. We then make sense of mixed data types with large language models, because legacy environments hardly store any clean, organised data. Our approach treats old systems not as a liability to be ripped out but as a source of valuable data and logic that AI can use, modernise, and reconnect to the rest of the business.

03

Operational Efficiency

A mainframe environment is expensive and labour-intensive. The IT team has to invest long hours or even days on repetitive, manual work, like scheduling batch jobs, allocating system resources, and triaging incidents whenever an issue is encountered, and writing up documentation after every fix. These tasks are such that one does not need to invest deep expertise every time, but they need to invest human attention and time. AI helps optimise operations with autonomous task handling, so instead of depending on the IT staff to manually assign system resources, manually schedule jobs, or sort incoming incidents by urgency, AI can handle them on its own. AI agents built by Q2M solutions don’t just answer the questions but also take actions, like: queuing jobs at the perfect time, assigning compute resources where they're needed most, automatically sorting incoming issues by severity.
