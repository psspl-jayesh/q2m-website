No binding agreements will be signed until otherwise agreed upon.

    The discovery process will take 2-4 weeks. A first production release – such as a solution for an AI assistant, document automation, or Copilot – will take 6-10 weeks. In case of more complex projects, we adopt a phased approach where you get an opportunity to assess results and business impact before scaling the solution further.
