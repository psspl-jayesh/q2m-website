Services

What we build

Seven practices, one team. Most projects combine two or three: an agent that reads documents, a dashboard that tracks it, and the cloud setup that keeps it running. Because one team covers all of them, you have one partner to call when something needs to change.

AI smart glasses app development

Smart glasses put instructions, cameras and an AI assistant in front of the people who work with their hands. We build apps that let a technician see the next step of an inspection, call a remote expert who sees what they see, and capture photos and video without stopping work. We build for Meta, XREAL, Snap Spectacles and MentraOS glasses, and we pick the device that suits the job, the budget and the environment.

  • Guided inspections and checklists, hands-free
  • Remote expert calls with live video from the wearer
  • Photo, video and voice notes tagged to the job
  • AI that reads labels, parts and damage in view

Agentic AI and intelligent automation

Many back-office tasks follow the same pattern: read something, check it against a rule or a system, decide, then update a record. We build agents that run these tasks end to end across email, documents, CRM and ERP. People stay in charge of the steps that carry risk, approving or correcting what the agent proposes.

  • Process mapping before any build
  • Human approval on high-risk steps
  • Audit trail for every action an agent takes

AI agent development

Custom agents for support, sales operations, claims, onboarding and internal knowledge. Each agent is connected to your own data and tools, scoped to a clear job, and tested against real examples from your business before it goes live.

  • Grounded answers from your documents
  • Connections to your CRM, ticketing and databases
  • Evaluation sets built from your real cases

Agentic commerce (ACP/UCP) readiness

Shoppers are starting to ask AI assistants to find and buy products for them. New protocols such as ACP and UCP define how those agents read a catalogue and complete a checkout. We review your product data, policies and checkout flow and make the changes needed so agents can find, compare and buy from you.

  • Catalogue and product data review
  • Checkout and policy changes for agent buyers
  • Testing with agent shopping flows

3D Gaussian splatting

Gaussian splatting turns ordinary phone or camera video into a photoreal 3D scene that runs in a web browser or headset. It suits property tours, site records, vehicle condition reports and training material, where a flat photo leaves too much out.

  • Capture guidance for your team
  • Browser and headset viewers
  • Measurements and annotations in the scene

MLOps

  • Model deployment and versioning

About Q2M

A young AI company built around delivery

Q2M Solutions is an Indian startup that turns AI ideas into working software. Our team combines engineers who build agents and data pipelines, developers who ship apps for smart glasses and XR headsets, and consultants who understand how operations teams actually work.

We started Q2M because we saw the same pattern again and again: companies run AI experiments that impress in a meeting and then stall. The model is rarely the problem. The hard parts are the connection to real systems, the handling of messy data, the approval steps that keep people comfortable, and the monitoring that keeps the system honest after launch. That is the work we focus on.

We work best with operations, IT and innovation leaders who have a specific process in mind and want to see it run with AI in weeks rather than plan it for a year. Whether you are a large enterprise, a global capability centre or a growing company, the approach is the same: pick one process, prove the value, then scale what works.

How we work

From first workshop to a system people use

Every project moves through the same four stages. You can stop after any stage and keep what we have produced.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We sit with the people who do the work today and map one process from start to finish: the inputs, the systems, the decisions and where time is lost. You get a written use-case brief with the expected benefit, the data needed and the risks.

    You get: use-case brief, process map, data review

  2. 02

    Pilot

    We build a working version on a small slice of real data and put it in front of a few users. The pilot is measured against the goals set in discovery, so the decision to go further is based on results.

    You get: working pilot, results report, production plan

  3. 03

    Production

    We harden the pilot: security review, connections to your live systems, monitoring, access control and training for the team. It goes live in stages, with a person approving critical steps until the numbers show it can run on its own.

    You get: live system, monitoring, user training

  4. 04

    Run and improve

    After launch we watch accuracy, cost and usage, retrain models when data changes and add the next use case. You can run it yourselves with our documentation, or keep us on a support plan.

    You get: support, retraining, a roadmap of next use cases

Why Q2M

What you can expect from us

We are a young company, so we keep our promises small and specific.

One process at a time

We start with a single workflow that matters to you, prove it works and then expand. Large AI programmes often stall when they try to change everything at once. A small win builds the trust and the data you need for the next one.

Your data stays yours

We build on your cloud or ours under clear terms, use your access rules and sign an NDA before we see anything sensitive. Nothing you share is used to train public models, and you can ask us to delete working copies at the end of a project.

People in the loop

Agents propose and people approve wherever a mistake would be costly. As confidence grows, you decide which steps can run on their own. The controls stay in your hands, not ours.

Plain reporting

Each stage ends with a short written report: what we built, what it measured, what it cost and what we recommend next. We avoid jargon and report the measures your finance and operations teams already use.
