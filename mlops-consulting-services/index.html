What Are MLOps Services?

MLOps services refer to the processes, tools, and practices that are used for designing and managing machine learning models throughout the model development process. The purpose of MLOps is to combine machine learning and operations to make the process reliable and automated.

MLOps turns models into systems that can support real business work and offer real business value. MLOps services can include a wide range of services, such as designing data pipelines, CI/CD for ML, model versioning, model monitoring and governance, infrastructural setup, and so on. The goal of a good MLOps platform is to ensure unaltered performance over time by tracking changes and responding quickly when performance shifts.

Q2M Solutions MLOps Process

To ensure valuable, sustainable, and practically valuable MLOps platforms, we followed a structured approach in developing our MLOps models. Each stage is carefully planned one after the other to ensure enterprise-grade performance.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    This is largely referred to as the assessment stage, where we gather information about your existing ML environment. This includes identifying your infrastructure, evaluating the scope of MLOps, understanding your business goals, and the technical challenges to be resolved. This helps us understand the workflow maturity and the areas of potential gaps that need to be fixed.

  2. 02

    Data Review and Planning

    Data is a pre-requisite for any modern-day development. At this stage, we review the quality, accuracy, and ease of accessing your existing business data. We identify if it's structured; otherwise, we organise, clean, and sort it to deliver practical value.

  3. 03

    Designing Architecture

    After understanding your business environment, we outline the architecture of the MLOps platform. This includes preparing the pipeline structure, setting up version control, finalising the deployment strategy, stating performance monitoring logic, and outlining strict governance policies.

  4. 04

    Pipeline Development

    We design the workflows essential for data transfer, model training, output validation, and updates. Automating this phase can help minimise errors and efforts for improved performance.

  5. 05

    Testing and Validation

    Before the system goes live, it's thoroughly tested and validated to ensure data consistency, its behaviour, reliability, and model performance.

  6. 06

    Deployment and Integration

    After validating the model, the next step in our MLOps consulting services is to deploy the model in the real environment and seamlessly connect it to the related systems. This can include CRMs, ERPs, APIs, cloud services, and other internal platforms.

  7. 07

    Monitoring and Optimisation

    Once the system is live in the market, we evaluate the model performance and enhancement needs. This is done by identifying performance bottlenecks, new upgrades, and workflow issues, if any.

  8. 08

    Training and Improvement

    As data and business demand shift, so should the MLOps platforms. Q2M Solutions serves as an end-to-end development partner helping businesses outline the retraining cycles, review real-time results, and improve system performance to ensure accuracy and relevancy.
