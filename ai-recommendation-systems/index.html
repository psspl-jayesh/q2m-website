What are AI Recommendation Systems?

AI recommendation systems refer to AI-powered software solutions that utilise data, machine learning, and behavioural trends to make relevant product, service, and content suggestions to users. They conduct a proper analysis of user buying patterns, browsing terms, and shifting user preferences. This provides future predictions, helping to deliver the right suggestions to the right user at the right time.

There are distinct ways to build these systems, varying by use cases. Different types of recommendation engines include content-based logic, modern AI recommendation systems, collaborative filtering, and so on. The best AI systems are the ones that are accurate as well as easy to integrate into your existing digital products with ease of management.

AI-Powered Recommendation Engines We Build

As an experienced AI development team, we build recommendation engines for distinct customer experiences. While some are customer-facing systems, some support internal workflows or content discovery. The right design of AI recommendation systems depends on the use case.

  • Content Recommendation Engines We design AI recommendation systems for media, learning platforms, and publishing hubs to help users easily find relevant articles, courses, videos, and other required content matching their interests.
  • Product Recommendation Engines These are the AI services that offer product suggestions based on user buying history, browsing patterns, category behaviour, and other relevant signals. They can be especially valuable in retail and eCommerce environments with discovery having a growing prominence.
  • Service Recommendation Engines In addition to products, some businesses make suggestions for services and packages. This type of recommendation engine assists users in carefully planning their next step by allowing them to simply navigate service catalogs and select relevant solutions.
  • Personalised Experience Engines Aside from products and content, AI recommendation systems can customise navigation pathways, page layouts, and user flows. The goal is to provide a tailored and responsive digital experience for everyone.
  • Cross-Sell and Upsell Engines Recommendation engines also suggest businesses with complementary or high-value options, which help to increase the average order value. These types of AI systems are frequently used in sales, retail, and subscription-based products.
